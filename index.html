WE BUILT THIS
BECAUSE WE
WERE BORED.

Not of Central Florida — of the same five tourist recommendations every time someone asked "what's there to do around here?"

Central Florida is one of the most visited places on earth, and somehow most people who live here — and most people who visit — only ever see a tiny sliver of it. The brochures send everyone to the same gates, the same International Drive restaurants, the same manufactured experiences.

But there's Wekiva Springs 30 minutes away. There's a food hall in downtown Sanford that beats anything on I-4. There are free art nights, underground bars, kayak trails through cypress forests, beaches an hour east, farmers markets, local music scenes, and neighborhoods full of life that most people drive straight past.

Central Florida is electric. You just have to know where to look.

407 Alive exists to make that easier. A free guide, updated weekly by locals, with no paid placements and no tourist board agenda. Just the spots we'd actually tell a friend about.

And beyond the guide — a community. Real meetups, real friendships, real adventures in the place we all call home. Because the best way to discover Central Florida is with people who love it as much as you do.

🙅
No Paid Placements

Every spot on this site earned its place. We don't accept money to feature businesses. If it's here, it's because locals love it.

🌍
Everyone Welcome

407 Alive is for everyone who lives in or loves Central Florida. The community is mixed, the events are accessible, and the vibe is always welcoming.
